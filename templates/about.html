About This Project

How RetailIQ Works

🛍️
01 — Overview
What Is RetailIQ?

RetailIQ is an AI-powered web platform that predicts the percentage of buying a product by the customer. It will help the retailers to understand the need of the customer so that they can fixed prices and discounts on the products.

🤖
02 — Technology
The ML Model

Using classification-based machine learning trained on real customer survey records, the model identifies patterns across all features to determine satisfaction likelihood.

🎯
03 — Mission
Our Goal

To give retailers a data-driven tool for understanding what drives customer satisfaction. RetailIQ helps to identify which factors most influence customer experience, predict customer and make smarter service improvement decisions backed by real survey data.
